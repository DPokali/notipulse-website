## Short description (80 characters)

## Full description

Swiped away a notification you wanted to read? NotiPulse lets you search and review notifications it has already saved on your Android device.

Enable notification access to start building your history. Find saved notifications by text or app, then explore notification counts and activity patterns.

SEARCH YOUR NOTIFICATION HISTORY
Review your saved notification log with search and filters. Find a message preview, reminder or update that you dismissed, provided NotiPulse recorded it before it disappeared.

UNDERSTAND YOUR NOTIFICATION ACTIVITY
See notification counts by app, hour, weekday and period. Explore activity patterns and identify the apps that interrupt you most. Use the optional daily digest to review your notification activity.

EXPORT YOUR RECORDS
Export your notification data to CSV or PDF when you want to review it separately. Exported files are generated on your device.

KEEP YOUR HISTORY LOCAL
Your notification data is stored on your device. No account is required. Privacy Mode skips saving notification titles and message text while retaining the technical information needed for statistics.

WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE YOU START
- Android notification access is required for the core features.
- History starts after setup. NotiPulse cannot recover previously dismissed notifications that it never saved.
- Text search requires saved text. Turning Privacy Mode off does not restore text that was never recorded.
- Android and the source app may restrict available notification content, including sensitive information.
- Retention depends on your cleanup settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local history.
- NotiPulse does not block, mute or modify notifications from other apps.

Use NotiPulse to keep a searchable notification history and make more informed choices about your notification settings.
